How it works

Four steps from artwork to upload

The easy path asks only what it needs. Every other setting starts at a sensible default, and the preview beside each step is a real frame of the final video.

Want more control? Turn on Advanced settings for the visualizer, colours, overlays, timing and export options.

Every shape

One release, framed for every feed

The same cover and song, laid out again for each platform’s shape — not a crop of one video.

Live preview

What you see is what renders

The preview beside every step is a frame of the real video, from the same FFmpeg pipeline the export uses — not a mock-up. Change a colour, move the logo, switch a platform: it updates.

  • Colour from the cover in one click, or pick any colour you like.
  • Drag the cover, logo, title and QR code into place, and rotate them.
  • Blur, cover size, bar count, sensitivity, fades — when you want them.
Visualizer

Pick a look by seeing it

Placements and styles are cards drawn in your colour, so you can tell them apart at a glance — and the preview shows the one you picked on your own artwork.

  • Bars on a musical scale: 60 Hz to 16 kHz, so the bass isn’t one tall bar on the left.
  • Smooth or snappy: set how fast the bars bounce.
Features

Built for releases, not one-offs

Singles, EPs and albums, with the same look on every video.

Batches

A whole EP in one render

Add every song of the release: each one gets its own video for every platform, and each can bring its own cover.

Private

Nothing leaves your computer

No account, no upload, no watermark, no queue. FFmpeg comes bundled on Windows and macOS.

OfflineNo sign-upNo watermark
Medley

A teaser for the whole release

Pick an excerpt of each song: they play back to back as one combined video per platform.

Duration

The whole song, or the hook

Render the full track, or drag the range to the part that sells it — and play the excerpt before you commit.

Templates

Your look, saved

Save the style as a template and load it on the next release: same colours, overlays and platforms, new cover and song.

Quality

Fast, and lossless where it counts

GPU encoding (NVENC, Quick Sync, AMF) with a software fallback, and uncompressed PCM audio by default.

Languages

English, Português, Español

Switch from the title bar, or follow the system’s language.
