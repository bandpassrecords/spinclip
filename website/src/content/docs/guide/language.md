---
title: Language
description: Using Spinclip in English, Portuguese or Spanish.
---

Spinclip speaks **English**, **Português** and **Español**. Pick one from the globe button in the title bar, or **System** to follow your computer's language.

![Spinclip in Portuguese](/resources/screenshots/release-type-pt.png)

The language changes immediately, with nothing lost from the release you're working on.
